The Gatherings

Join us at The Chateau Inn for unforgettable days filled with adventure, entertainment, and royal hospitality!

The Royal Feast

Join us for a grand medieval-inspired banquet featuring delicious food, candlelit tables, roasted meats, fresh bread, and desserts. Enjoy an evening of royal hospitality!

Every other evening at seven to nine pm.

The Phoenix Lantern Festival

Celebrate beneath the stars at our magical lantern festival! Enjoy glowing lantern displays, live music, storytelling, and beautiful phoenix decorations throughout the courtyard, Light refreshments provided.

Every saturday at Nine pm.

The Knight's Challenge

Step into the arena and experience a medieval knight's tournament! Guests can participate in friendly mock sword battles using padded swords and shields. Challenge the champion and cheer on your favorite knights!

Mon-Wend-Fri at Three to Five pm
