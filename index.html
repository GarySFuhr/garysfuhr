Welcome to The Chateau Inn

Welcome to The Chateau Inn, where rustic charm meets modern comfort. Nestled in the beautiful countryside, our ranch-style inn offers guests a relaxing getaway surrounded by scenic landscapes, spacious horse pastures, and elegant accommodations. Whether you're looking to enjoy horseback riding, unwind in a peaceful atmosphere, or explore the surrounding countryside, The Chateau Inn offers a memorable experience for everyone. Inspired by the spirit of adventure found in fantasy worlds, our inn combines Western hospitality with unique decorative touches, including our signature phoenix emblem. Come stay, ride, and discover your next adventure at The Chateau Inn!
